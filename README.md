# ccint Signal Desk — demo

Read-only snapshot of the ccint portal: Canada-related cybersecurity discussion
collected from Bluesky and public RSS feeds, with topic trends, the latest
fortnight, CVE / CISA KEV links, and Canadian ransomware victims matched to posts.

Live page: https://astronautrronearth.github.io/ccint-demo/

Code: https://github.com/astronautrronearth/ccint

Individuals and unprofiled accounts are pseudonymised and their post links
removed; accounts profiled as media, organisations or intel feeds keep their
handle. Topic figures cover posts through the date shown in the page header.
